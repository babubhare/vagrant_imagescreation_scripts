initramfs Creation
-------------------

$ arm-linux-gcc --static init.c -o init

$ file init

$ echo init|cpio -o --format=newc > initramfs

$ qemu-system-arm -M vexpress-a9 -kernel linux-6.6.11/arch/arm/boot/zImage -dtb linux-6.6.11/arch/arm/boot/dts/arm/vexpress-v2p-ca9.dtb -append "console=ttyAMA0,115200" -nographic -initrd initramfs
