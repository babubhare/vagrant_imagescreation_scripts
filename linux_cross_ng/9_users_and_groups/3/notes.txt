Creating groups
--------------------

Group names are stored in /etc/group

$ man 5 group

There is one line per group consisting of four fields separated by colons.

--> Name of group

---> Group password, usually an x character, indicating that there is no group password

---> GID or group ID

---> An optional list of users who belong to this group, separated by commas

Eg.

root:x:0:
lwl:x:1:
