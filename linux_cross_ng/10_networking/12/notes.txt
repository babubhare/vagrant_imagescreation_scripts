Transferring files using netcat
--------------------------------

On receiving side: $nc -l -p 1234 > my_file.bin

On sender side: cat my_file.bin | nc <dest_ip_addr> 1234

This causes nc to create TCP connection to <dest_ip_addr> on port 1234 and send everything it gets on standard input

Known Flaws
-----------

Sometimes nc doesn't end at EOF and just hang waiting for next data. In this case, just break with CTRL-C on both ends.
Then verify content is transferred correctly using md5sum
